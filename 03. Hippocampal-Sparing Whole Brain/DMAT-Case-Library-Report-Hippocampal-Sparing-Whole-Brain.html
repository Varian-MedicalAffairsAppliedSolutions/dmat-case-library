1. Case overview

Summary. 6 plans were evaluated: 5 DMAT variants and 1 reference plan.

Clinical background and rationale. Hippocampal-sparing whole-brain treatment planned to 30 Gy in 10 fractions. The comparison examines whole-brain coverage and hippocampal dose across modulation levels.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 8FFF; 2 arcs; modulation −1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: TB 4Arc — 6FFF, 4 arcs.

Goal setup. The DMAT source reports contained 26 provided goals per plan, with priority identifiers, goal thresholds and acceptable-variation fields. Derived and automatic objectives were listed separately. For DMAT -1, examples were PTV_3000 V30.00 Gy > 95.0 % (P2.1); Hippocampus D100.0 % ≤ 6.50 Gy (P1.1).

Comparison methods. Dose colorwash, DVHs, individual endpoint–time plots and the saved 8-endpoint scorecard were compared. All saved dose factors were 1.0; no additional rescaling was applied. Scores summarize the selected endpoints. DMAT timing came from matched metrics; reference timing used an RT Plan control-point estimate.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DMAT -1’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 5–34.5 Gy color scale.
