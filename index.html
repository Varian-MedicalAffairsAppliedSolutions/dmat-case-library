DMAT Case Library

The DMAT plan library presents examples across anatomical sites, prescription schedules, beam energies, and modulation settings, alongside reference plans where available. Reports include planning parameters, field geometry, dose distributions, dose–volume histograms, selected dosimetric endpoints, and delivery-time data. The examples document technical planning characteristics and do not establish clinical benefit or comparative superiority.
