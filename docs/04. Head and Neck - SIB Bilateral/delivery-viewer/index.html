Geometry & timing assumptions

The dual-layer profile uses 306 / 400 mm source distances, 80 mm leaf height and 230 mm rounded tips. Leaf boundaries and positions come from RP. The generic profile uses a 400 mm layer distance.

Without metrics: estimated timing uses RP MU, nominal dose rate, and illustrative limits of 6°/s gantry, 15°/s collimator, 3°/s couch yaw and 25 mm/s physical leaf speed. It is not a delivery log.

Transition tracking

Choose one or more subjects to guide camera aim during view switches, guided tours, and custom-camera transitions. Multiple subjects use their combined center. Targets uses visible target contours; unavailable subjects are skipped. Leave all unchecked for no tracking.

Links between different shots use a continuous pullback and arc with a gradual change of focus. Choose Auto duration in the keyframe editor to give the move time by shortening its hold. Preset holds remain unchanged until edited; tracking also guides CAX moves between matching presets. You can override the selection on each keyframe’s next transition.
