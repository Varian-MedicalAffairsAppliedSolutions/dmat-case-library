1. Case overview

Summary. Thirteen plans compare twelve DMAT variants with one HyperArc-EDGE reference. Saved comparative scores range from 5.488 to 8.005 out of 10; delivery durations range from 75.7 to 390.3 seconds. Individual target and organ-at-risk endpoints are shown alongside the aggregate score.

Clinical background and rationale. This single-target vestibular schwannoma case compares plans for a 13 Gy prescription delivered in one fraction. The comparison examines target coverage, conformity, dose fall-off, cochlea and brainstem dose, and delivery duration under the recorded planning setup.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT-6FFF: 6FFF, 4 arcs; modulation -1, 0, +1, +3. DMAT-8FFF: 8FFF, 4 arcs; modulation -1, 0, +1, +3. DMAT-10FFF: 10FFF, 4 arcs; modulation -1, 0, +1, +3. Reference setup: HyperArc-EDGE: 6FFF, 4 arcs.

Goal setup. 13 provided goals per DMAT plan. Goal thresholds and priorities are listed in the planning tables; derived and supporting objectives are separate. Example from DA231: Brainstem D0.1 % < 9.44 Gy (P1.1). Comparative scoring uses the saved custom scorecard, “CI and GI - Vestibular Schwannoma - 13 Gy x 1”, with six endpoints: RTOG conformity index, gradient index, mean cochlea dose, PTV D99%, PTV V100%, and brainstem D0.03 cc. Saved thresholds, score curves and achieved values are retained in Scorecard details.

Comparison methods. The figures compare the saved dose colorwash, DVHs, aggregate quality–time scores and individual endpoint–time results. DMAT plans use a dose multiplier of 1.000; the HyperArc-EDGE reference uses 1.030. DMAT: Matched delivery metrics; HyperArc-EDGE: RT Plan control-point estimate. Durations do not represent total room time.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DA229’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 3–21 Gy color scale.
