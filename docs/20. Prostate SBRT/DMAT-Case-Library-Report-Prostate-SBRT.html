1. Case overview

Summary. Seven plans compare six DMAT modulation variants with one VMAT reference. Saved comparative scores range from 9.761 to 9.994 out of 10; delivery durations range from 33.6 to 94.8 seconds. Individual target and organ-at-risk endpoints are shown alongside the aggregate score.

Clinical background and rationale. This case compares prostate SBRT plans with a dominant intraprostatic lesion (DIL) boost. The saved prescription is 50 Gy to GTV primary, 40 Gy to CTVp and 36.25 Gy to PTV, delivered in five fractions. The comparison examines how modulation changes target coverage, organ-at-risk dose and delivery duration under the recorded planning setup.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 10FFF, 1 arc; modulation -2, -1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: VMAT: 10FFF, 2 arcs.

Goal setup. 22 provided goals per DMAT plan. Goal thresholds and priorities are listed in the planning tables; derived and supporting objectives are separate. Example from DA277: Urethra V42.00 Gy < 50.0 % (P1.1).

Comparison methods. Compared the dose colorwash, DVHs, quality–time graph and individual endpoint–time graphs. Dose normalization factors: 1. DMAT: Matched delivery metrics; VMAT: RT Plan control-point estimate. Durations do not represent total room time.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DA277’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 10–54.5 Gy color scale.
