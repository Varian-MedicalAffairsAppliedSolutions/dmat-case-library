1. Case overview

Summary. 8 plans were evaluated: 7 DMAT variants and 1 reference plan.

Clinical background and rationale. Whole-breast treatment prescribed 40.05 Gy with a 48 Gy integrated boost in 15 fractions. The comparison examines breast and boost coverage alongside heart and lung dose.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 8FFF; 1 arc; modulation −3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: VMAT — 6FFF, 5 arcs.

Goal setup. The DMAT source reports contained 20 provided goals per plan, with priority identifiers, goal thresholds and acceptable-variation fields. Derived and automatic objectives were listed separately. For DMAT -3, examples were PTV_High V45.60 Gy ≥ 95.0 % (P1.2); Breast right Dmax < 0.76 Gy (P1.1).

Comparison methods. Dose colorwash, DVHs, individual endpoint–time plots and the saved 18-endpoint scorecard were compared. All saved dose factors were 1.0; no additional rescaling was applied. Scores summarize the selected endpoints. DMAT timing came from matched metrics; reference timing used an RT Plan control-point estimate.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DMAT 0’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 5–52.5 Gy color scale.
