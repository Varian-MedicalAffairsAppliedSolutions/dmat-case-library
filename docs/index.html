These plan reports showcase the technical capabilities of the DMAT algorithm across different treatment sites and planning scenarios. Plans shown here were generated by certified dosimetrists planners and physicists internally from Varian Medical Affairs and Clinical Applications. Each report compares DMAT plans at selected modulation levels with the available reference plans, bringing together dose distributions, dosimetric scores, and delivery-time information. All plan inputs are visible in the report. They are intended for technical review and education, rather than claims of clinical benefit.

For education and demonstration only. Not intended for clinical use or clinical decision-making.
