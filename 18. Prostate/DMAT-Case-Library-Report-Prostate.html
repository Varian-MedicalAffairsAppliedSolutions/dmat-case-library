1. Case overview

Summary. Seven plans compare six DMAT modulation variants with one VMAT_TB reference. Saved comparative scores range from 8.874 to 9.588 out of 10; delivery durations range from 25.4 to 132.9 seconds. Individual target and organ-at-risk endpoints are shown alongside the aggregate score.

Clinical background and rationale. This prostate case compares plans for a 79.2 Gy prescription delivered in 44 fractions, with Prostate and PTV recorded as the target structures. The comparison examines how modulation changes target coverage, organ-at-risk dose and delivery duration under the recorded planning setup.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 10FFF, 1 arc; modulation -2, -1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: VMAT_TB: 10FFF, 2 arcs.

Goal setup. 23–24 provided goals per DMAT plan. Goal thresholds and priorities are listed in the planning tables; derived and supporting objectives are separate. Example from DA208: PTV D0.03 cm3 ≤ 83.16 Gy (P1.1). Comparative scoring uses the saved custom scorecard, “Prostate 79.2Gy”, with 23 endpoints covering target dose and coverage, rectum, bladder, femoral heads, bowel, penile bulb and Ring. Saved thresholds, score curves and achieved values are retained in Scorecard details.

Comparison methods. Compared the dose colorwash, DVHs, quality–time graph and individual endpoint–time graphs. Dose normalization factors: 1. DMAT: Matched delivery metrics; VMAT_TB: RT Plan control-point estimate. Durations do not represent total room time.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DA206’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 20–87 Gy color scale.
