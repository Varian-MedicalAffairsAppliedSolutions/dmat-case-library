1. Case overview

Summary. 8 plans were evaluated: 7 DMAT variants and 1 reference plan.

Clinical background and rationale. Locally advanced lung treatment prescribed 60 Gy in 30 fractions. The comparison examines lung, esophageal and heart dose alongside target coverage and duration.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 8FFF; 1 arc; modulation −3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: 2 Arc VMAT — 6FFF, 2 arcs.

Goal setup. The DMAT source reports contained 24 provided goals per plan, with priority identifiers, goal thresholds and acceptable-variation fields. Derived and automatic objectives were listed separately. For DMAT -3, examples were PTV-Plan D95.0 % ≥ 100.0 % (P2.2); Heart Dmean < 7.00 Gy (P1.1).

Comparison methods. Dose colorwash, DVHs, individual endpoint–time plots and the saved 14-endpoint scorecard were compared. All saved dose factors were 1.0; no additional rescaling was applied. Scores summarize the selected endpoints. DMAT timing came from matched metrics; reference timing used an RT Plan control-point estimate.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DMAT -3’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 5–68 Gy color scale.
