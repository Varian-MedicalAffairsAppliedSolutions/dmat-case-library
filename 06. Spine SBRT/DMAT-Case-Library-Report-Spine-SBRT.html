1. Case overview

Summary. 5 plans were evaluated: 4 DMAT variants and 1 reference plan.

Clinical background and rationale. Spine targets prescribed 27 Gy to the PTV and 32.4 Gy to the GTV in three fractions. The comparison examines GTV and PTV coverage alongside spinal-cord dose.

Plan generation. DMAT plan generated for: Accela. DMAT: 10FFF; 1 arc; modulation 0, +1, +2, +3. Reference setup: VMAT_Edge — 10FFF, 3 arcs.

Goal setup. The DMAT source reports contained 10 provided goals per plan, with priority identifiers, goal thresholds and acceptable-variation fields. Derived and automatic objectives were listed separately. For DMAT 0, examples were PTV V27.00 Gy ≥ 90.0 % (P2.2); SpinalCord_PRV D0.03 cm3 < 20.60 Gy (P1.1).

Comparison methods. Dose colorwash, DVHs, individual endpoint–time plots and the saved 21-endpoint scorecard were compared. All saved dose factors were 1.0; no additional rescaling was applied. Scores summarize the selected endpoints. DMAT timing came from matched metrics; reference timing used an RT Plan control-point estimate.

Colorwash sampling details

Every dose is evaluated on DMAT +3’s patient-coordinate dose grid before CT slices are sampled every 5 mm from its isocenter. Native grids that differ are trilinearly resampled. The in-plane display and superior–inferior slice range are restricted to the selected reference plan’s 40% prescription-dose bounding volume plus 50 mm in every direction; CT slices outside that range are not embedded. Each colorwash has a slim slice slider beneath it; moving any slider advances every plan synchronously. The compact zoom buttons, pointer drag, and reset remain synchronized and persist while changing slices. Mouse-wheel scrolling continues to move the report page. All plans use the confirmed 10–36 Gy color scale.
